<p className="mt-4">Over time I have taken an interest in competitive programming, which led me to C++, since it is
more or less the standard language in that world. The more I learnt it and the more I used it, the more I came to like
the low-level control it gives you. That made me want to try C++ outside of contest problems, so I wrote a clone of the
in-memory key-value store that needs no introduction - Redis. The original is written in C in a procedural style, while
my version tries to reproduce the main functionality in C++ with a mix of procedural and object-oriented code.</p>
<p className="mt-4">I say a mix because I am not convinced that OOP is the right abstraction for everything, especially
after taking a small dive into functional programming through &apos;Functional Programming in Scala&apos; by Paul Chiusano
and R&uacute;nar Bjarnason. That said, I did appreciate the encapsulation C++ gives you, and the standard library.</p>
<p className="mt-4">This is the first project I have written at this kind of networking and low level, and I have to
admit it was genuinely exciting to work on. It is the direction I hope to keep going in, assuming functional programming
does not distract me first.</p>
